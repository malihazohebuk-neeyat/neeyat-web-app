# Demo Guide

Use this sequence for an Innovator Founder / Envestors-style product demonstration:

1. Open the homepage and explain Neeyat's positioning.
2. Click **Explore the Demo** and show the demo accounts.
3. Use consumer quick login.
4. Open product search and apply category, price and ethical-score filters.
5. Open a product detail page.
6. Explain retailer price comparison and affiliate disclosure.
7. Show ethical-score breakdown and data-confidence limitations.
8. Go to the consumer dashboard and change personal values.
9. Show influencer marketplace and dashboard.
10. Show business dashboard and improvement recommendations.
11. Show admin dashboard, moderation and commercial model.
12. Open methodology and explain that data is simulated.
