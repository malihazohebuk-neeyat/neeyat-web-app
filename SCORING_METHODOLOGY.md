# Ethical Score Methodology

The prototype ethical score is calculated programmatically using these weighted components:

| Component | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Environmental impact | 30% |
| Labour and sourcing | 25% |
| Governance and transparency | 20% |
| Product and packaging responsibility | 15% |
| Certification and evidence quality | 10% |

Score bands:

- 80-100: Leading
- 65-79: Strong
- 50-64: Developing
- 35-49: Limited
- 0-34: High concern

Confidence depends on demo variables such as evidence points, evidence recency, third-party verification and self-reported data.

The score is illustrative. It is not an external certification, legal audit, financial recommendation or guarantee of ethical performance.
