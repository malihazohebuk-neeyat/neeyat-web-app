# Neeyat Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence

Neeyat helps people make ethical money decisions with clarity, confidence and commercial realism.

Positioning: AI-powered ethical commerce intelligence platform.  
Tagline: Ethical Money Decisions.  
Primary market: United Kingdom.

## Brand Promise

Compare the price. Understand the impact. Choose with intention.

Neeyat should feel like a trusted intelligence layer between consumers, retailers, ethical influencers and transparent businesses.

## Brand Personality

- Calm
- Intelligent
- Transparent
- Premium but accessible
- Practical
- Evidence-led
- Values-aware without being judgmental

Avoid language that sounds preachy, exaggerated or unsupported.

## Visual Identity

### Logo

Use:

- `assets/neeyat-logo-web.png` for the web header and UI.
- `assets/neeyat-logo-transparent.png` only for large-format layouts.
- `assets/favicon.png` for browser favicon and compact identity.

The original transparent logo has a large square canvas. The cropped `neeyat-logo-web.png` is preferred for web usage because it displays reliably in navigation.

## Typography

Use modern, readable sans-serif typography. The interface should prioritise clarity over decoration.

Recommended feel:

- Large confident headings.
- Clear sentence-case labels.
- Compact data tables.
- No excessive letter spacing except small uppercase tags.

## UI Principles

- Put the product experience first, not a marketing-only landing page.
- Pair every ethical score with an explanation and confidence level.
- Use restrained cards, clear tables and transparent disclosures.
- Avoid unsupported live-data claims.
- Make commercial model pages clear but not aggressive.
- Keep sponsored placements visibly labelled.

## Voice and Messaging

Use:

- "Illustrative demonstration data"
- "AI-ready recommendation prototype"
- "Ethical score methodology"
- "Data confidence"
- "Price and impact comparison"

Avoid:

- "Certified ethical"
- "Guaranteed sustainable"
- "Government approved"
- "Real-time prices" unless live feeds are implemented
- "Fully ESG compliant"

## Core Messaging Pillars

1. Price clarity: show total cost, delivery and value.
2. Ethical evidence: show score components, confidence and limitations.
3. Personal fit: adapt to different budgets and values.
4. Commercial maturity: demonstrate subscriptions, affiliate income, influencer sharing, B2B SaaS and sponsored listings transparently.

## Product Screens

Key screens should reinforce:

- Search and comparison.
- Ethical score breakdown.
- Retailer price comparison.
- Personal values match.
- Influencer recommendations.
- Business analytics.
- Admin moderation.
- Commercial model.

## Prototype Disclaimer

Use visible disclaimers wherever relevant:

"Functional Prototype - Demonstration Data. Prices, scores, analytics and earnings are illustrative and not live feeds."
